儿童个人信息保护声明

欢迎使用 Mango Pie English（以下简称“本应用”）。 我们重视儿童及其家长、监护人的隐私与个人信息保护。 本声明旨在说明本应用的运行方式，以及儿童使用本应用时 涉及的个人信息保护事项。

重要说明： 本应用设计为离线英语学习应用。根据当前版本的设计， 本应用不主动收集、上传或向第三方传输儿童的个人信息， 不提供语音录制或语音分析功能，也不展示广告。

一、适用范围

本声明适用于 Mango Pie English 当前版本提供的英语学习功能。 如果未来增加联网、账户、云同步、语音识别、广告或其他 可能涉及个人信息处理的功能，我们将根据适用法律法规 更新相关说明，并在适当情况下取得必要的授权或同意。

二、我们如何处理个人信息

本应用主要通过应用内的学习内容帮助儿童学习英语。 按照当前版本的设计：

  • 本应用不要求儿童注册账户或提供真实姓名。
  • 本应用不要求儿童提供电话号码、电子邮件地址或家庭住址。
  • 本应用不主动收集、上传或向我们的服务器发送儿童个人信息。
  • 本应用不提供语音录制、语音分析或在线语音处理功能。
  • 本应用不包含广告展示或广告行为追踪功能。
  • 本应用不使用第三方广告服务或第三方分析服务。
  • 本应用不将儿童的个人信息出售、出租或用于定向广告。

上述说明仅针对本应用当前版本的功能和行为， 不代表设备操作系统、应用商店或设备上其他软件 不会根据其自身规则处理相关技术信息。

三、设备权限

本应用当前的英语学习功能不需要通过麦克风录制儿童的声音， 也不需要为了语音分析而访问麦克风。 如果未来版本增加需要设备权限的功能，我们会说明相关用途， 并在适用法律要求的情况下取得必要授权。

四、第三方服务与信息共享

本应用当前版本的核心学习功能设计为离线运行， 不依赖在线语音识别服务、广告网络或云端学习记录服务。 我们不会通过本应用主动向第三方共享儿童个人信息。

如果未来引入第三方服务，我们将评估其隐私保护措施， 说明相关服务的用途及可能处理的信息，并根据适用法律 履行告知、授权和其他必要义务。

五、信息存储与安全

本应用当前版本不主动将儿童个人信息上传至我们的服务器， 也不提供云端个人学习档案功能。 设备本地的应用数据可能由操作系统按照其自身机制管理。 我们建议家长和监护人妥善管理儿童使用的设备及其访问权限。

六、家长与监护人的权利

家长和监护人可以协助儿童了解安全使用应用的方式， 并管理儿童使用设备的权限。 如果您对本应用的儿童隐私保护有疑问或希望提出建议， 可以通过本声明末尾提供的联系方式与我们联系。

七、儿童隐私保护声明的更新

如果本应用的功能、数据处理方式或适用法律法规发生变化， 我们可能更新本声明。更新后的版本将在本页面发布， 并标明相应的更新日期。建议家长和监护人定期查看本页面。

Children's Privacy Protection Statement

Welcome to Mango Pie English (the "App"). We value the privacy and protection of children and respect the rights of their parents and legal guardians. This statement explains how the App operates and how children's personal information is addressed in the current version.

Important: The App is designed as an offline English-learning application. In its current version, the App does not actively collect, upload, or transmit children's personal information to third parties. It does not provide voice recording or voice-analysis features and does not display advertisements.

1. Scope

This statement applies to the English-learning features provided by the current version of Mango Pie English. If future versions introduce online services, user accounts, cloud synchronization, speech recognition, advertising, or other features that may involve personal information, we will update the relevant disclosures and obtain authorization or consent where required by applicable law.

2. How the App Handles Personal Information

The App is designed to help children learn English using learning content within the application. In the current version:

  • The App does not require children to create accounts or provide their real names.
  • The App does not request telephone numbers, email addresses, or home addresses.
  • The App does not actively collect, upload, or send children's personal information to our servers.
  • The App does not provide voice recording, voice analysis, or online speech-processing features.
  • The App does not display advertisements or perform advertising-behavior tracking.
  • The App does not use third-party advertising or analytics services.
  • The App does not sell, rent, or use children's personal information for targeted advertising.

These statements describe the current functionality of the App. They do not mean that the device operating system, app store, or other software on the device cannot process technical information under their own policies.

3. Device Permissions

The current English-learning features do not require the App to record children's voices through the microphone or access the microphone for voice analysis. If a future version introduces features requiring device permissions, we will explain their purposes and obtain authorization where required by applicable law.

4. Third-Party Services and Sharing

The core learning features in the current version are designed to operate offline. They do not depend on online speech-recognition services, advertising networks, or cloud-based learning-record services. We do not actively share children's personal information with third parties through the App.

If third-party services are introduced in the future, we will assess their privacy practices, disclose their purposes and relevant information processing, and fulfill applicable notice, authorization, consent, and other legal requirements.

5. Information Storage and Security

The current version does not actively upload children's personal information to our servers and does not provide cloud-based personal learning profiles. Application data stored locally on a device may be managed by the operating system according to its own mechanisms. Parents and guardians are encouraged to manage children's devices and access permissions appropriately.

6. Rights of Parents and Guardians

Parents and guardians can help children learn to use the App safely and manage the permissions available on their devices. If you have questions or suggestions concerning children's privacy protection in the App, please contact us using the contact details below.

7. Changes to This Statement

We may update this statement if the App's features, information practices, or applicable laws change. The updated version will be published on this page with a corresponding revision date. Parents and guardians are encouraged to review this page periodically.
